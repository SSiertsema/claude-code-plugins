Outreach hooks

  2. ISO 27001:2022 just achieved. Recent compliance milestone — relevant for security-tooling vendors, audit partners, and procurement-focused buyers (gemeenten care). [6]
  3. LVBB / officiële-publicaties koppeling. Recent product feature ingesting Dutch official publications — hook for regulatory-data vendors, BAG/PDOK partners, or competitors in regulatory tech. [14]
  4. Big municipal logos. Amsterdam (4-year SaaS) and Arnhem are flagship references — use these as analogue case studies when pitching other Dutch municipalities (Utrecht, Rotterdam, Den Haag are the obvious gaps). [9]
  5. The AFWC + 6 corporaties deal (~300k units) is unusually large for a founder-led NL proptech and a strong proof point — useful for outreach into other Dutch corporatie federations (DAEB-segment, regional federations). [10]
